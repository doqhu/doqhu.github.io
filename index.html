What I'm building

Eloqo is a gym for your speaking muscles.

Eloqo is a mobile AI speech coach. You record a short speaking exercise, such as an articulation drill, a monologue, shadowing or reading aloud. It scores clarity, fluency, vocabulary and confidence against the transcript, then names one thing to fix next.

  1. 01

    Record a short take. One to three minutes, from tongue twisters to retelling a text from memory.

  2. 02

    See instant numbers. Pace, filler words and hesitation pauses are calculated on the device, free and without an account.

  3. 03

    Ask for a coach review. Gemini on Vertex AI scores the take, annotates the transcript and suggests the next exercise.
